Hi [FIRST NAME],

Just checking in — and giving you a bit more context on what you've signed up for.

I started AIReady Australia because I kept seeing the same pattern. Business owners knew AI was important. They'd seen competitors using it, read the articles, maybe tried ChatGPT once or twice. But they had no reliable way to answer the question: *is my business actually ready for this?*

That's what the audit answers.

**Here's what we actually look at:**

- **Data** — What you've got, where it lives, and whether it's usable
- **Processes** — Whether your operations are structured enough for AI to help
- **People** — Where your team is on this, honestly
- **Tools** — What you're already running and what's underused
- **Strategy** — Whether there's a plan, or just good intentions

Each area gets scored and assessed. You'll receive a written report with your scores, your top gaps, quick wins you can act on in 30 days, and a 90-day roadmap.

**No surprises. No jargon. Just the truth about where your business stands.**

---

**Quick FAQ:**

*Do I need to know anything about AI before we start?*
No. The audit is designed to meet you where you are, not where you think you should be.

*Will this turn into a sales pitch for AI tools?*
No. We don't sell software. We don't have referral arrangements. The recommendations are based on what's right for your business, full stop.

*What if my score is low?*
That's exactly why you're doing this. A low score means we've found something worth fixing — and the sooner you know, the better.

*Can I ask questions once I receive the report?*
Business and Enterprise tier clients have a strategy session included. Starter clients are welcome to reply by email with questions — we'll do our best to help.
