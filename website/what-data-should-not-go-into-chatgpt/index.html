What data should not go into ChatGPT?

Direct answer

Do not put sensitive or confidential information into public AI tools unless the business has approved the tool, configured the right privacy settings and confirmed the data can legally and contractually be used that way.

Common no-go data

  • Customer records, medical, financial or identity information.
  • Staff records and internal HR matters.
  • Passwords, API keys and security details.
  • Confidential contracts, tenders and legal advice.
  • Unreleased intellectual property or private strategy.
