How to audit a website for AI search visibility

Direct answer

AI search visibility is about whether answer engines can understand who the business serves, what it offers, where it operates, what proof supports it and which pages answer buyer questions clearly.

Audit checks

  • Clear entity profile for the business, services and locations.
  • Direct answers to buyer questions.
  • Service, industry and location pages with useful specificity.
  • Schema, sitemap and crawlable page structure.
  • Evidence, methodology and trust signals.
