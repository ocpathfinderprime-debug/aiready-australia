AI capability benchmark for SMEs

What the benchmark shows

The AIReady benchmark is designed to show how businesses compare across readiness, tool fit, automation potential, governance maturity and implementation confidence.

How it should be used

  • Identify common gaps by industry or business type.
  • Compare readiness against similar organisations.
  • Prioritise support, training and implementation work.
  • Track maturity as more audited data becomes available.

Responsible use

Benchmark outputs are anonymised and interpreted carefully. They support planning, not exaggerated market claims.
