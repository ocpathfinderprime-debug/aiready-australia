ChatGPT vs Copilot for business

Direct answer

Microsoft Copilot is often strongest for businesses already operating deeply in Microsoft 365. ChatGPT can be more flexible for drafting, analysis, ideation and workflow support across varied tools. The right answer depends on the business environment and risk controls.

Decision factors

  • Current Microsoft 365 dependency.
  • Types of workflows being improved.
  • Data sensitivity and permission model.
  • Integration needs.
  • Training effort and adoption likelihood.
