How to measure AI ROI in small business

Direct answer

AI ROI should be measured against a clear business outcome: hours saved, faster response times, reduced software spend, better conversion, fewer manual errors or increased delivery capacity.

Simple ROI model

  • Monthly hours saved multiplied by loaded labour cost.
  • Revenue uplift from faster follow-up, quoting or content production.
  • Software cost reduction from removing duplicated tools.
  • Risk reduction from governance, permission and data controls.
  • Implementation cost, training time and subscription spend.
