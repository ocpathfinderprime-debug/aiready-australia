AIReady FAQ knowledgebase

Common questions

  • Is this consulting or an audit? It is a structured audit that can lead into consulting or implementation support.
  • Who is it for? Small and medium businesses that want practical AI adoption guidance.
  • What do I receive? A scored report, recommendations, risk notes and next-step priorities based on the selected package.
  • Do you recommend tools? Yes, but only after reviewing workflow fit, risk, adoption effort and ROI.
  • Is sensitive data required? No public page requires sensitive data. Any sensitive audit context should be handled through approved intake and privacy controls.
