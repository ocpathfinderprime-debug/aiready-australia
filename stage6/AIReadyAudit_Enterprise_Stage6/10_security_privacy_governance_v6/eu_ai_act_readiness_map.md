# EU AI Act Readiness Map

## Purpose

Provide global expansion screening for customers or partners operating in or selling into the EU.

## Screening questions

- Is the system prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk or minimal-risk?
- Does it involve biometric identification, employment, education, essential services, migration, justice, law enforcement or critical infrastructure?
- Is AI-generated content presented to the public?
- Is a GPAI model integrated into the solution?
- Who is provider, deployer, importer or distributor?

## AIReady boundary

This is an operational readiness aid, not legal advice. EU AI Act classification and compliance decisions should be reviewed by qualified counsel.
