# Australian Responsible AI Practices Matrix V6

## Purpose

Map AIReady Audit governance review outputs to Australian safe and responsible AI guidance.

## Practical control areas

- accountability and ownership
- risk management
- data governance
- transparency and disclosure
- human oversight
- monitoring and incident response
- supply-chain and vendor review
- staff training and acceptable use

## Audit outputs

- governance readiness score
- missing policy list
- risk register
- recommended controls
- priority roadmap
