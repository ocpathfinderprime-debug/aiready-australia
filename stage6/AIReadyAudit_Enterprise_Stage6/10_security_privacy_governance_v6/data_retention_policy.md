# Data Retention Policy

## Defaults

- Free score inputs: retain for 24 months unless deletion requested.
- Paid audit evidence: retain for 12 months after report delivery unless contract states otherwise.
- Reports: retain for 36 months for customer access and audit trail.
- Logs: retain for 12 months unless security investigation requires longer.
- Benchmark data: retain only in anonymised or aggregated form.

Confirm final retention terms with legal review before production.
