# Incident Response Runbook Stage 6

## Severity levels

- SEV1: confirmed data breach, cross-tenant exposure, credential compromise.
- SEV2: service outage, failed payment flow, major report error.
- SEV3: degraded service, non-critical job failures.

## Response steps

1. Triage and assign incident commander.
2. Contain the issue.
3. Preserve logs and evidence.
4. Notify internal stakeholders.
5. Assess legal/customer notification obligations.
6. Remediate.
7. Run post-incident review.
8. Update controls and tests.
