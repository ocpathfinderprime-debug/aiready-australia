# Stage 6 Launch Command Operating System

## Purpose

Coordinate product, website, engineering, delivery, sales, AI-search, support and OC Prime integration work through one operating cadence.

## Command roles

- Executive owner: approves launch gates, commercial claims and high-risk changes.
- Product commander: owns scope, product roadmap and release readiness.
- Engineering lead: owns runtime, API, database, MCP and deployment quality.
- OC Prime lead: owns event mesh, dashboards, approvals and cross-app routing.
- Search lead: owns SEO, AI citation lab, content quality and authority building.
- Delivery lead: owns audit QA, report quality and customer outcomes.
- Partner lead: owns reseller and white-label onboarding.
- Security lead: owns access, incident response, MCP risk and data boundaries.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: command review, blockers, release gate status.
- Tuesday: product and engineering delivery review.
- Wednesday: AI-search/citation review and content decisions.
- Thursday: sales, partner and customer success review.
- Friday: QA, incident review, metric scorecard and next-week commitments.

## Operating rule

No new feature enters public launch unless it has an owner, success metric, rollback path, support note, telemetry event and OC Prime visibility.
