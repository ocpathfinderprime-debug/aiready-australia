# Investor Update Pack

## Purpose

Investor Update Pack supports the Stage 6 commercialisation path for AIReady Audit.

## Core strategy

- Win Australia first through specific AI readiness, tools, automation and AI website visibility positioning.
- Build proof through sample reports, case studies, benchmark insights and directory/entity citations.
- Convert demand through free readiness score, paid audits and implementation sprints.
- Expand through trusted partners and white-label workspaces only after quality gates are proven.
- Enter global markets with localised pages, partner delivery and compliance-aware positioning.

## Execution actions

1. Define target buyer segment.
2. Build message and proof assets.
3. Run outbound, content and partner experiments.
4. Track conversion and delivery quality.
5. Feed learnings back into OC Prime and product roadmap.
