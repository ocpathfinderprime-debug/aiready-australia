# Data Dictionary Stage 6

## Core entities

- Tenant: customer, internal business unit, partner or white-label workspace.
- Lead: person or organisation entering the funnel.
- Readiness score: scored result from free or paid diagnostic.
- Audit workspace: delivery record for a paid audit.
- Citation prompt: standard test prompt for AI-search visibility.
- Citation observation: one result from an AI citation test.
- Partner: reseller, referral partner or white-label operator.
- Report review: QA record before customer delivery.
- Audit log event: immutable record of material system actions.
