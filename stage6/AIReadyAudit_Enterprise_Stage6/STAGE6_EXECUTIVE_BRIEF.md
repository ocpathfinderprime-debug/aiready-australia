# Stage 6 Executive Brief

## Strategic position

Stage 5 created the launch operating system. Stage 6 makes it commercially measurable and scalable.

The goal is not to simply add more pages or tools. The goal is to build a system where every buyer signal, audit workflow, AI citation, partner opportunity and implementation task is visible inside OC Prime and governed by measurable quality controls.

## Commercial thesis

AIReady Audit can win by becoming the most specific, trusted and operationally useful provider for these categories:

- AI readiness audit for Australian businesses
- AI tools audit for small and medium businesses
- AI automation opportunity audit
- AI website visibility audit for AI search and chat recommendations
- AI governance review for practical business adoption
- Partner-delivered and white-label AI readiness assessments
- SME AI capability benchmark data

## Stage 6 differentiators

1. **Audit product, not consulting ambiguity** — clear packages, entitlements, SLAs, QA gates and report deliverables.
2. **AI-search measurable** — AI visibility is treated as a measurable citation system, not a vague GEO claim.
3. **OC Prime-native** — every high-value signal becomes a routed event, dashboard tile or approval workflow.
4. **MCP-ready** — AI clients can call governed tools without exposing unsafe write access.
5. **Data moat** — anonymised readiness benchmarks compound over time.
6. **Partner-scale** — white-label and reseller delivery can expand without breaking report quality.

## Commercial release stages

- **Stage 6A:** Staging release and internal pilot.
- **Stage 6B:** Founder-led public launch in Australia.
- **Stage 6C:** Partner pilot with 2-5 trusted delivery partners.
- **Stage 6D:** Marketplace/MCP app submission readiness.
- **Stage 6E:** Global market templates for New Zealand, Singapore, UK, Canada and US.
