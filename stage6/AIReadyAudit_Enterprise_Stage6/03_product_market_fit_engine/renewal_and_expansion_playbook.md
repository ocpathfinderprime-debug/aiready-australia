# Renewal and Expansion Playbook

## Expansion paths

- Starter Audit → Business Audit
- Business Audit → Implementation Sprint
- Website Visibility Audit → Monthly AI citation monitoring
- Governance Review → Quarterly AI governance review
- Enterprise Assessment → multi-team roadmap and implementation portfolio

## Renewal triggers

- roadmap due date approaching
- new tools introduced
- staff growth
- compliance event
- website relaunch
- AI citation decline
- competitor citation gain
