# AIReady SME AI Capability Index

## Purpose

Create a defensible data product from anonymised audit and readiness score data.

## Public outputs

- quarterly AI readiness benchmark
- industry-level readiness score ranges
- most common automation opportunities
- most common governance gaps
- tool category adoption trends
- regional AI adoption insights

## Data rules

- no named customer without consent
- suppress small cell counts
- aggregate by industry, size and region
- remove direct identifiers
- publish methodology
- maintain opt-out process
