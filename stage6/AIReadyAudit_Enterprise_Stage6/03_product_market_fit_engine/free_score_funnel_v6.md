# Free Score Funnel V6

## Funnel steps

1. Visitor lands on service, industry or location page.
2. Visitor starts free AI readiness score.
3. Form collects company size, industry, workflows, current tools, AI usage, data risk, automation appetite and implementation capacity.
4. System produces score and top opportunities.
5. Lead is routed to OC Prime with qualification score.
6. Follow-up sequence offers paid audit, website visibility audit or implementation sprint.

## Required events

- readiness_score_started
- readiness_score_completed
- package_recommended
- lead_qualified
- sales_followup_requested
- audit_purchased

## Conversion targets

- landing page to score start: 5-12%
- score start to completion: 55-75%
- score completion to consultation or purchase: 5-15%
