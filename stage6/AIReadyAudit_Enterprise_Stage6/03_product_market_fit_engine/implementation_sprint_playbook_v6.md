# Implementation Sprint Playbook V6

## Purpose

Turn audit recommendations into installed workflows, trained staff and measurable adoption.

## Sprint structure

- Week 1: confirm priorities and select one to three workflows.
- Week 2: configure tools and automations.
- Week 3: test with staff and refine.
- Week 4: handover, training and measurement plan.

## Outputs

- implementation backlog
- configured workflow or tool setup
- staff guide
- risk controls
- adoption metrics
- next-quarter roadmap
