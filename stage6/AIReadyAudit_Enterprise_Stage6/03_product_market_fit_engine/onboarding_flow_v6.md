# Customer Onboarding Flow V6

## Flow

1. Purchase or qualified lead created.
2. Customer receives welcome email and intake link.
3. Intake captures business profile, workflows, tools, priorities and constraints.
4. Evidence room opens for screenshots, documents or process notes.
5. Audit workspace is created and assigned to delivery owner.
6. QA owner is assigned before report drafting.
7. Final report is delivered with next-step options.

## Required customer-facing clarity

- what data to provide
- what not to provide
- turnaround time
- refund and revision terms
- report limitations
- privacy and AI use disclosure
