# Pricing Experiment Matrix

## Guardrails

- Do not discount the audit below delivery cost.
- Keep the free score valuable but incomplete enough to justify paid analysis.
- Avoid enterprise fixed pricing until discovery scope is known.

## Experiments

| Product | Baseline | Test | Metric |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter Audit | $497 | $697 | paid conversion and margin |
| Business Audit | $1,497 | $1,997 | sales cycle and close rate |
| Website Visibility Audit | $997 | $1,497 | conversion and report effort |
| Implementation Sprint | $3,000+ | retainer | attach rate |
| Partner Workspace | custom | tiered seat/platform fee | partner margin |
