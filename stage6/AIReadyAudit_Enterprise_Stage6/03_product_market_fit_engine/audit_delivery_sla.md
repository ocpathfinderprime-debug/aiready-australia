# Audit Delivery SLA

## Standard SLA

- Starter audit: 5 business days after completed intake.
- Business audit: 7 business days after completed intake.
- Enterprise assessment: scoped in statement of work.
- Website visibility audit: 5 business days after access/evidence supplied.

## SLA exclusions

- incomplete intake
- inaccessible systems
- missing evidence
- customer delay
- scope expansion

## SLA events

- audit_workspace_created
- intake_completed
- report_draft_ready
- qa_review_completed
- report_delivered
- sla_breach_warning
- sla_breach
