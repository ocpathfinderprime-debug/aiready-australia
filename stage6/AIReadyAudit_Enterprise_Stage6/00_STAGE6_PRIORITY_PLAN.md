# Stage 6 Priority Plan

## Mission

Make AIReady Audit the leading Australian and globally scalable AI readiness, AI tools, AI automation and AI-search visibility audit platform.

## Priority sequence

1. **Command and scope control** — freeze the Stage 6 release train, gates, owners and operating cadence.
2. **Runtime staging** — merge the web, API, worker, MCP and OC Prime bridge runtime into a staging repository.
3. **Data and event foundation** — apply database migrations, RLS, audit logs, warehouse models and event contracts.
4. **Website V6 authority layer** — publish commercially focused, AI-citable pages with entity clarity and structured data.
5. **AI citation operating system** — run weekly citation checks across Google, Bing/Copilot, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
6. **OC Prime dry-run integration** — route leads, score events, audit status, approval requests and citation findings to command center dashboards.
7. **MCP and ChatGPT app readiness** — deploy read-only tools, add draft tools, then unlock write tools behind approval tokens.
8. **Delivery quality engine** — process pilot audits through human QA, score reconciliation, report review and customer feedback.
9. **Commercial scale** — launch founder-led sales, partner pilots, white-label candidates and enterprise procurement packs.
10. **Benchmark product** — begin anonymised AI readiness benchmark collection for a defensible data asset.

## Stage 6 exit criteria

- Public site has V6 homepage, service, industry, location, comparison, sample report and methodology pages live.
- Free readiness score and AI website visibility audit funnel are functional in staging.
- OC Prime receives and displays structured AIReady events in dry-run mode.
- MCP read-only tools pass auth, telemetry and tool-risk tests.
- 10 pilot audits pass delivery QA with customer approval.
- Weekly citation lab can report citation presence, source pages, grounding queries and competitor mentions.
- Partner/white-label pilot has scoped onboarding, entitlements and QA controls.
