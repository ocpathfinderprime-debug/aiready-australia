# Benchmark Index Strategy V6

## Objective

Turn anonymised readiness scores and audit findings into the AIReady SME AI Capability Index.

## Product forms

- public quarterly report
- gated industry benchmark report
- API for aggregated benchmark comparisons
- partner benchmark dashboard
- PR/media insights

## Defensibility

The index becomes stronger as more audits and free scores are completed. It supports authority, citations, sales proof and product improvement.
