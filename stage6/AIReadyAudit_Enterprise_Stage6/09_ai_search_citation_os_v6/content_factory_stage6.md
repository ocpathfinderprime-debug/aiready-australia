## Production workflow

1. Select prompt gap from citation lab.
2. Define search intent and buyer stage.
3. Draft page with direct answer, example and evidence.
4. Review claims and data.
5. Add internal links and schema.
6. Publish and submit to index.
7. Track citation and conversion impact.
