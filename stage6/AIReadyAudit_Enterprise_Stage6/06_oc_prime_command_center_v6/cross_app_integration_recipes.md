# Cross-App Integration Recipes

## AIReady → CRM app

Trigger: readiness score completed and lead qualified.  
Action: create or update CRM contact with source, package recommendation and follow-up task.

## AIReady → Content app

Trigger: citation gap detected.  
Action: create content brief using target prompt, missing page type and competitor references.

## AIReady → Finance app

Trigger: audit purchased or partner commission due.  
Action: create invoice, revenue event or payout task.

## AIReady → Delivery app

Trigger: audit workspace created.  
Action: assign delivery owner, due date and QA reviewer.

## AIReady → Governance app

Trigger: high-risk AI use case identified.  
Action: create AI impact assessment task.
