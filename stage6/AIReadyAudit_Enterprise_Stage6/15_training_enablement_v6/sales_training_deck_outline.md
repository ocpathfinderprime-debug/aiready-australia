# Sales Training Deck Outline

## Purpose

Train internal team members, partners and support staff to sell, deliver and support AIReady Audit consistently.

## Key messages

- AIReady provides practical fixed-scope audits.
- Recommendations are human-reviewed.
- No ranking, ROI, legal or compliance guarantees are made.
- Data boundaries and privacy are central to the delivery model.
- OC Prime is the command center for events, approvals and delivery visibility.
