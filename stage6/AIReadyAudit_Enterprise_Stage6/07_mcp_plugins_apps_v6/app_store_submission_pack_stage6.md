# App Store Submission Pack Stage 6

## App name

AIReady Audit

## Short description

Assess business AI readiness, AI opportunities, AI tool fit and AI website visibility.

## Long description

AIReady Audit helps business owners and operators understand where AI can save time, which tools fit, what risks need governance and how to create a 90-day implementation roadmap. The app can also help website owners review whether AI answer engines can understand and cite their services.

## Safety notes

- Free readiness scores are informational.
- Paid audit reports require human review.
- The app does not provide legal, financial, cybersecurity or medical advice.
- High-risk write actions require human approval.
- Customer evidence is tenant-isolated.
