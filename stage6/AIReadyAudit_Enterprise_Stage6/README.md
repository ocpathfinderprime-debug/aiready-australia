Purpose: **Market-scale enterprise production, AI-search citation leadership, OC Prime orchestration, MCP/app marketplace readiness and global commercialisation.**


Stage 6 converts the Stage 5 launch operating system into a market-scale, enterprise-grade commercialisation platform.
It focuses on production hardening, AI-search citation operations, OC Prime cross-app orchestration, MCP/app marketplace readiness, partner and white-label execution, benchmark data products, SRE controls and global expansion.

This pack is designed to be merged into a production repository and connected to OC Prime as a governed app mesh module. It creates the assets required to operate AIReady Audit as a commercial product line rather than a single marketing site.

## Deployment principle

1. Freeze Stage 6 scope and define launch-command ownership.
2. Merge runtime code and database migrations into staging.
3. Deploy read-only MCP and OC Prime dry-run integrations first.
4. Publish Website V6 authority pages and AI-readable entity profiles.
5. Run citation measurement and conversion analytics weekly.
6. Process 10-25 pilot audits through the delivery quality gate.
7. Activate partner and white-label pilots only after SLOs, QA and support controls pass.

## Not a live deployment

This package creates the developer, operator and commercial handover assets. It does not connect production credentials, deploy the site, charge customers, submit marketplace apps, or modify OC Prime runtime. Production deployment requires repository, hosting, database, identity, payment, analytics, email, telemetry and OC Prime endpoint access.
