# Delta from Stage 5

Stage 5 provided a launch-grade operating system. Stage 6 adds market-scale execution depth.

## Added in Stage 6

- launch-command operating cadence with war-room dashboard specification
- Website V6 AI authority layer and answer-entity profile
- stronger citation scoring model and AI-search operating loop
- runtime code additions for citation scoring, benchmark indexing and OC Prime bridge operations
- warehouse/star-schema and benchmark data product models
- OC Prime memory-boundary policy and cross-app shared ontology
- marketplace submission assets and app-risk register
- partner and white-label quality controls
- SRE/SLOs, OpenTelemetry, backup/restore, load test and release-train assets
- Australian responsible AI mapping, EU AI Act readiness map and MCP security controls
- enablement material for sales, delivery, support and partner certification

## Not changed

- Stage 6 does not replace the Stage 5 runtime; it hardens and extends it.
- Human review remains mandatory for paid audit reports and material customer-facing recommendations.
- High-risk write tools remain approval-gated.
- No claim is made that AI systems can be forced to recommend the site; the system optimises for crawlability, usefulness, authority, citations and measurement.
