# Content Quality Bar V6

A page cannot be published unless it passes all checks.

## Required checks

- Solves a real buyer question.
- Includes direct experience or a concrete example.
- Avoids unsupported superiority claims.
- Contains a plain-language answer in the first 150 words.
- Links to methodology, pricing, sample report and related services.
- Includes author/reviewer metadata where relevant.
- Is factually reviewed and dated.
- Has a unique purpose and is not a thin city/keyword clone.
- Includes structured data where applicable.
- Has a defined conversion goal.
