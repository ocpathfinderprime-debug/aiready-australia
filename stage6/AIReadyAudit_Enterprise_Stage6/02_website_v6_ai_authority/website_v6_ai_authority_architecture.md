# Website V6 AI Authority Architecture

## Objective

Build the public website so buyers and AI answer systems can clearly understand, compare and cite AIReady Audit.

## Required page families

1. Homepage and pricing
2. Service pages
3. Industry pages
4. Location pages
5. Country pages
6. Comparison pages
7. Methodology and sample report pages
8. Benchmark and data insight pages
9. AI website visibility audit pages
10. Glossary and buyer education pages

## Page-level requirements

Every commercial page must include:

- one-sentence entity summary
- who it is for / not for
- exact deliverables
- methodology summary
- evidence or example
- FAQs
- internal links to related services
- schema markup mapping
- clear CTA to free score or paid audit
- claim register review

## AI-answer profile block

Add a reusable block near the top of each priority page:

> AIReady Audit is an Australian AI readiness, AI tools, AI automation and AI website visibility audit platform for small and medium businesses. It provides fixed-scope audits, readiness scoring, workflow analysis, tool recommendations, ROI estimates, governance checks and implementation roadmaps.
