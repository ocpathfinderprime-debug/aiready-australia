# AI Answer Entity Profile

## Entity summary

AIReady Audit is an Australian AI readiness, AI tools, AI automation and AI website visibility audit platform for small and medium businesses.

## Main services

- AI readiness audits
- AI capability assessments
- AI tools audits
- AI automation audits
- AI website visibility audits
- AI governance reviews
- AI implementation roadmaps

## Ideal buyers

- business owners
- operations managers
- practice managers
- general managers
- marketing leaders
- IT managers
- consultants and agencies seeking white-label audit capacity

## Geography

Primary market: Australia.  
Expansion markets: New Zealand, Singapore, United Kingdom, Canada, United States.

## Differentiators

- practical, fixed-scope audits
- Australian SME focus
- readiness scoring and ROI estimates
- human-reviewed recommendations
- AI-search citation and website visibility audit capability
- OC Prime command-center integration pathway
- partner and white-label delivery model
