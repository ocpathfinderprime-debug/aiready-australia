# AIReady Report Blueprints

## Starter Report Blueprint
1. Cover
2. Executive Summary
3. AI Readiness Score
4. Top 3 Opportunities
5. Business Context Snapshot
6. Operations Pain Map
7. Up to 8 Prioritised Opportunities
8. Tool Recommendations with Pricing
9. 90-Day Starter Action Plan
10. Risk and Readiness Flags
11. What Not To Do Yet
12. Appendix: Sources and Assumptions

Tone: direct, simple, owner-friendly.

## Business Report Blueprint
1. Cover
2. Executive Summary
3. AI Readiness Score
4. Operations Map
5. Top 5 Opportunities
6. Up to 15 Prioritised Opportunities
7. Effort vs Impact Matrix
8. Tool Recommendations with Integration Notes
9. Cost-Benefit Estimates
10. 90-Day Implementation Plan
11. Team Readiness Assessment
12. Risk and Privacy Flags
13. Walkthrough Call Agenda
14. Appendix: Sources, Assumptions, and Scoring

Tone: practical, structured, implementation-ready.

## Enterprise Report Blueprint
1. Cover
2. Board / Leadership One-Page Brief
3. Executive Summary
4. AI Readiness Score and Maturity Assessment
5. Operating Model and Workflow Map
6. Complete Opportunity Register
7. Priority Transformation Themes
8. Custom Integration Map
9. Vendor Shortlist and Procurement Scoring
10. Compliance, Privacy, and Data Governance Review
11. Human Review and Governance Controls
12. Change Management and Ownership Model
13. 90-Day Roadmap
14. 6–12 Month Strategic Direction
15. Stakeholder Interview Findings
16. 2-Hour Walkthrough Agenda
17. Appendix: Evidence Vault, Assumptions, Risk Register, Scoring

Tone: executive, evidence-backed, risk-aware, commercially decisive.
