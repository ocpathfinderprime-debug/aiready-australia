# AIReady Opportunity Scoring Model

## Score Each Opportunity From 1–5

### A. Business Value
1 = minor convenience  
3 = meaningful time/cost/customer impact  
5 = major strategic, revenue, cost, or capacity improvement

### B. Implementation Ease
1 = complex/custom/multi-system change  
3 = moderate setup or process change  
5 = easy, low-cost, fast to implement

### C. Evidence Strength
1 = weak or speculative  
3 = credible but limited evidence  
5 = strong evidence from official sources, case studies, vendor documentation, or direct client data

### D. Data and Compliance Safety
1 = high-risk or sensitive data risk  
3 = manageable with controls  
5 = low-risk or no sensitive data involved

### E. Team Readiness
1 = likely resistance or poor ownership  
3 = workable with training  
5 = clear owner and team acceptance

### F. 90-Day Suitability
1 = not realistic in 90 days  
3 = partial pilot possible  
5 = realistic quick win

## Weighted Score
`Priority Score = (Business Value x 30%) + (Implementation Ease x 20%) + (Evidence Strength x 15%) + (Data Safety x 15%) + (Team Readiness x 10%) + (90-Day Suitability x 10%)`

## Classification
- 4.25–5.00 = Priority 1: implement first
- 3.50–4.24 = Priority 2: strong candidate
- 2.75–3.49 = Priority 3: plan or pilot later
- 2.00–2.74 = Watchlist
- <2.00 = Do not recommend now

## Mandatory Fields Per Opportunity
- opportunity title
- problem solved
- client evidence from intake
- proposed AI/tool/process solution
- recommended owner
- estimated effort
- estimated cost band
- expected benefit
- risk flags
- required data
- human-review requirement
- implementation steps
- 90-day milestone
- evidence links
- priority score
