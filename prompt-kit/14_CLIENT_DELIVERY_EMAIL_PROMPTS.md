# Client Delivery Email Prompts

## Starter Delivery Email

```text
Draft a concise delivery email for a Starter AI Readiness Audit.

Include:
- report attached;
- top 3 opportunities briefly named;
- reminder that the report is designed for practical 90-day action;
- no walkthrough call or follow-up support unless separately purchased;
- invitation to reply if they want help implementing.
```

## Business Delivery Email

```text
Draft a delivery email for a Business AI Readiness Audit.

Include:
- report attached;
- top 5 opportunity themes;
- link/request to book the 1-hour walkthrough call;
- mention 30-day Q&A by email;
- ask them to review the 90-day implementation plan before the call.
```

## Enterprise Delivery Email

```text
Draft a delivery email for an Enterprise AI Readiness Audit.

Include:
- report attached;
- board brief attached or included;
- request to book the 2-hour walkthrough;
- mention 60-day follow-up Q&A;
- note any critical governance or privacy flags requiring leadership attention;
- ask who should attend the walkthrough.
```
