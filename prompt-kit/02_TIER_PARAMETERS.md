# AIReady Tier Parameters

## Starter — $497
Designed for sole traders and small teams needing a clear starting point.

### Client Profile
- Usually 1–10 staff.
- Lower operational complexity.
- Limited budget.
- Needs quick wins and simple tool choices.

### Research Depth
- Intake-led research only.
- Basic industry and competitor context.
- Tool scan focused on low-cost, proven tools.
- No deep custom integration architecture.
- No stakeholder interview unless separately approved.
- Evidence target: 10–15 quality sources.
- Tool comparison target: 6–10 tools.
- Opportunity cap: up to 8 prioritised opportunities.

### Deliverables
- Executive summary.
- AI readiness score.
- Top 3 opportunities.
- Operations pain-point map.
- Up to 8 prioritised opportunities.
- Named tool recommendations with indicative pricing.
- 90-day starter action plan.
- Basic risk/readiness flags.
- No walkthrough call.
- No follow-up support unless separately sold.

### Value Logic
Starter clients pay for clarity and direction, not a full transformation design. The report must avoid over-engineering. The highest value is identifying the easiest wins with the least tool spend, least process disruption, and fastest owner-led implementation.

## Business — $997
Designed for growing businesses with operational complexity and multiple functions.

### Client Profile
- Usually 10–50 staff.
- More workflows, handovers, customer touchpoints, and tools.
- Needs prioritisation, integrations, ownership, and realistic implementation sequence.

### Research Depth
- Expanded workflow research.
- Sales, admin, customer service, reporting, finance, and operations review.
- Competitor and market context review.
- Tool comparisons with integration notes.
- Data/privacy screen.
- Evidence target: 20–35 quality sources.
- Tool comparison target: 12–20 tools.
- Opportunity cap: up to 15 prioritised opportunities.
- Create walkthrough agenda and Q&A prep.

### Deliverables
- Executive summary.
- AI readiness score.
- Operations map.
- Up to 15 prioritised opportunities.
- Effort vs impact matrix.
- Tool recommendations with integration notes.
- Cost-benefit estimates.
- 90-day implementation plan.
- Risk and readiness flags.
- Team readiness assessment.
- 1-hour walkthrough call pack.
- 30-day follow-up Q&A tracking notes.

### Value Logic
Business clients pay for translation from analysis into execution. They need not only what to do, but who should own it, what order it should happen in, what tools connect to what systems, and where implementation could fail.

## Enterprise — $1,997
Designed for larger, multi-location, compliance-sensitive, or integration-heavy businesses.

### Client Profile
- Usually 50–200 staff.
- Multiple locations, departments, data stores, or decision layers.
- Higher compliance, change-management, integration, and governance risk.

### Research Depth
- Comprehensive intake and stakeholder interview preparation.
- No arbitrary cap on opportunity register.
- Deep workflow, data, integration, risk, and governance review.
- Custom tech-stack integration map.
- Vendor shortlisting and procurement scoring.
- Compliance and data privacy review.
- Change management and AI governance recommendations.
- Evidence target: 40–75 quality sources.
- Tool comparison target: 20–35 tools/vendors.
- Interview plan: up to 3 stakeholders.
- Board/leadership summary required.

### Deliverables
- Board/leadership 1-page executive brief.
- Comprehensive AI readiness report.
- Complete opportunity register.
- Custom integration map.
- Compliance and data privacy risk review.
- Vendor shortlist.
- Governance and human-review recommendations.
- Effort, value, risk, dependency, and sequencing model.
- 90-day implementation roadmap plus 6–12 month direction.
- 2-hour walkthrough call pack.
- 60-day follow-up Q&A tracking notes.
- Scoping call notes before audit begins.

### Value Logic
Enterprise clients pay for reduced strategic risk and implementation confidence. The report must be more than a list of tools: it must map people, process, data, systems, compliance, ownership, governance, and staged execution.
